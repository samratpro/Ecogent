AI token and its concern:

A token is a piece of words, including symbols, that a large Language model takes as input and output

When we want to use an AI agent, token cost matters

============
Not only small business owners, marketers, developers, or designers; recently, Uber and NVIDIA, like these giant company thinking about AI Agent token cost cause they replace humans with AI coding, but they found it costs more than human replace

Literature Review: Industry Efforts
========================================
The industry is actively researching ways to improve AI performance and reduce model costs. However, current solutions, such as Model Distillation and RAG, still rely on stateless loops that do not give AI agents persistent “muscle memory.”

I also found that most companies are currently focused on improving AI agent capabilities, reducing LLM model costs, and exploring new computing systems such as quantum computers. However, they still pay less attention to the overall cost of running AI agents.
